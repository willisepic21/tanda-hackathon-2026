Prove you're human by folding a protein.

FoldPass replaces "click every traffic light" with a second or two of real computation: a search for low-energy folds of an HP lattice protein. Every result is computed independently by two devices and only counts when they match bit-for-bit.

  1. Challenge. The server sends a signed work unit (sequence, seed, attempts) and a small proof-of-work puzzle.
  2. Compute. A Web Worker solves the puzzle and runs a deterministic Monte-Carlo folding search.
  3. Verify. The server checks the fold in microseconds: no self-collisions, correct H–H contact count, consistent energy histogram.
  4. Replicate. The same unit goes to a second device. Matching results are validated; mismatches go to a tie-breaker and cost the liar trust.
  5. Pass. The host site's backend redeems a one-time signed token via /api/siteverify.

Honest caveat: a few seconds per CAPTCHA is a tiny amount of science, and bots have cheap CPUs. The PoW and rate limits are the bot gate. The folding is a bonus, kept honest by replication and known-answer spot checks.
